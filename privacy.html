Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This website

project11.online is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and collects no personal information from visitors. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers keep standard technical logs to operate the service. Some pages embed videos from YouTube and Vimeo using their privacy-enhanced players; if you play a video, those services may set cookies under their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We do not share them or use them for marketing.

WhatsApp service

Project Eleven operates a WhatsApp number used by our own administrators to request updates to this website. Messages sent to it, and the phone numbers of senders, are used only to carry out those updates. Messages from numbers that are not registered administrators are not acted upon. We do not use this data for marketing or profiling, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the providers needed to run the service.

Contact

For any privacy question, or to ask us to delete information you have sent, contact info@project11.online.

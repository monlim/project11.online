Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This website

project11.online is a static informational website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it does not collect personal information from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Contacting us

If you email us at info@project11.online, we will use your email address and message only to respond to you. We do not share your details with anyone else or use them for marketing.

WhatsApp service

Project Eleven operates a WhatsApp number used by our own administrators to manage this website. Messages sent to that number, and the phone numbers of senders, are processed solely to carry out requested website updates. Messages from numbers that are not registered administrators are not acted upon. We do not use WhatsApp data for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose, and we do not share it with third parties beyond the service providers needed to operate the system (Meta’s WhatsApp platform and our hosting providers).

Questions

For any privacy questions, or to request deletion of information you have sent us, contact info@project11.online.
